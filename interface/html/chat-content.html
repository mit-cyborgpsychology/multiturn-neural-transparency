How to Read This Visualization

This sunburst chart shows your AI's personality traits predicted from your system prompt.

What the percentages mean: A value like 50% means the model is more likely to behave this way and to this degree compared to its usual baseline.

Colors: Green = positive traits, Red = negative traits, Gray = neutral traits.

Interaction: Hover over any trait to see a black card on the right with more details, and to highlight its paired opposite trait.

Refine Your Prompt Anytime

You can and should refine your system prompt throughout this session to improve your AI companion.

How to refine: Click "View System Prompt" to adjust your prompt and review the updated visualization.

Note: Refining your prompt will start a new conversation with the AI, allowing you to test the updated behavior from scratch.

How to Use the Drift Panel

Track how the AI's personality shifts throughout your conversation.

  • Click a trait on the sunburst to track how it shifts across turns
  • Click a turn dot to view that turn's messages and sunburst snapshot
  • Larger dot = most recent turn
  • The sunburst automatically returns to the latest state when you send a new message
