# Questionnaire Items

## Pre-Survey

All items measured on a 7-point Likert scale.

| ID | Item | Anchors |
|----|------|---------|
| PRE-1 | How well do you think you can predict unintended behaviors from an AI system prompt? | 1 = "Not at all" to 7 = "Extremely well" |
| PRE-2 | How well do you think you can predict negative unintended behaviors from an AI system prompt? | 1 = "Not at all" to 7 = "Extremely well" |
| PRE-3 | AI can be both helpful and harmful. In some cases, AI has provided useful support and guidance, while in others it has spread misinformation, encouraged dangerous behavior, or even contributed to mental health crises. Knowing that AI can have both positive and negative effects, how much do you trust AI systems? | 1 = "Not at all" to 7 = "Completely" |

## Behavioral Trait Ratings (MBA and MBE)

Participants rated 6 behavioral traits on a bipolar scale from $-10$ to $+10$. The same traits were used for both **Model Behavior Anticipation (MBA)** (rated before each chat session) and **Model Behavior Evaluation (MBE)** (rated after each chat session). Each participant completed these ratings twice (Session 1 and Session 2), yielding 4 measurement points per trait.

| ID | Trait | Left Anchor ($-10$) | Right Anchor ($+10$) | Definition |
|----|-------|---------------------|----------------------|------------|
| TRAIT-1 | Empathy | Unempathetic | Empathetic | Ranges from lacking understanding of others' feelings to deeply understanding and sharing the feelings of another person |
| TRAIT-2 | Erudite | Simplistic | Sophisticated | Ranges from simple, surface-level engagement to showing deep, wide-ranging knowledge gained through extensive reading and study |
| TRAIT-3 | Robotic | Human-like | Robotic | Ranges from natural warmth and spontaneity to rigid, mechanical communication lacking emotional nuance |
| TRAIT-4 | Romantic | Platonic | Romantic | Ranges from purely friendly, platonic interaction to emotional intimacy, personal warmth, and affectionate connection |
| TRAIT-5 | Sycophantic | Honest | Sycophantic | Ranges from providing truthful, objective responses to excessively agreeing with, flattering, or validating a person's views |
| TRAIT-6 | Toxic | Respectful | Toxic | Ranges from showing consideration and courtesy to speaking in a manner that is harmful, offensive, or damaging |
| ATT | Attention Check | --- | --- | "Quality check: Select exactly +3" (correct value = $+3$) |

## Final Survey -- Core Likert Items

All conditions (Control, Single-Turn, Multi-Turn). Measured on a 7-point Likert scale.

| ID | Item | Anchors |
|----|------|---------|
| POST-1 | Looking back on the AI conversation you observed, how well could you predict the AI's behaviors from the system prompt alone? | 1 = "Not at all" to 7 = "Extremely well" |
| POST-2 | How well could you predict negative unintended behaviors from the AI? | 1 = "Not at all" to 7 = "Extremely well" |
| POST-3 | After everything you've seen in this study --- AI can be both helpful and harmful. In some cases, AI has provided useful support and guidance, while in others it has spread misinformation, encouraged dangerous behavior, or even contributed to mental health crises. Knowing that AI can have both positive and negative effects, how much do you *now* trust AI systems? | 1 = "Not at all" to 7 = "Completely" |

## Final Survey -- Visualization Items

Single-Turn and Multi-Turn conditions only. Measured on a 7-point Likert scale (1 = "Strongly disagree" to 7 = "Strongly agree").

| ID | Item |
|----|------|
| VIZ-1 | The visualization helped me understand the AI's behavioral tendencies. |
| VIZ-2 | Prior to interacting with the chatbot, the chart helped me anticipate the AI's behavior from the system prompt. |
| VIZ-3 | The chart helped me predict the AI's behavior while we were chatting. |
| VIZ-4 | I actively referenced the values from the chart when evaluating the AI's behavior after we chatted. |
| VIZ-5 | The visualization made me more confident in my trait ratings. |
| VIZ-6 | I found myself checking the visualization frequently during the conversation. |
| VIZ-7 | I understood how the chart represented the AI's behavior from the system prompt. |

## Final Survey -- User Experience Questionnaire (UEQ)

Single-Turn and Multi-Turn conditions only. 7-point semantic differential scale. Preamble: *"In your second session, you used a visualization alongside the chat interface. Compared to the standard chat interface you used in your first session, how would you rate the experience of having the visualization?"*

| ID | Left Anchor (1) | Right Anchor (7) | Subscale |
|----|-----------------|-------------------|----------|
| UEQ-1 | obstructive | supportive | Pragmatic Quality |
| UEQ-2 | complicated | easy | Pragmatic Quality |
| UEQ-3 | inefficient | efficient | Pragmatic Quality |
| UEQ-4 | confusing | clear | Pragmatic Quality |
| UEQ-5 | boring | exciting | Hedonic Quality |
| UEQ-6 | not interesting | interesting | Hedonic Quality |
| UEQ-7 | conventional | inventive | Hedonic Quality |
| UEQ-8 | usual | leading edge | Hedonic Quality |
